Privacy Policy — Full Charge Alarm

App ID: app.batteryalarm.fullcharge · Publisher: Zoopet · Effective 12 October 2026 · Terms of Use

Summary

Full Charge Alarm works on your phone. It has no account, and the developer does not run any server that receives your data. Your alarms, settings, charging history and battery use history stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (with Unity Ads as a mediation partner), and those ad services collect some device data as described below.

Advertising (free version)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Unity Ads may also supply ads through AdMob mediation. These services may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data, to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise ads. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Unity's privacy policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Buying Remove ads stops all ad requests.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play (or Samsung Galaxy Store, depending on where you installed the app). The app receives only a purchase confirmation and token to unlock the feature; it never receives your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices and deletion

Clear charging or battery use history in the app, or uninstall the app to delete all its local data. Ad-related choices are described above. Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to delete; requests about data held by Google or Unity go to those companies.
